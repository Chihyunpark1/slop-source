# RSLOP Contributor Airdrop — Evaluation Criteria

## Status: not yet confirmed

The evaluation criteria for the RSLOP contributor airdrop are **not yet
confirmed**. Nothing in this document is a promise of a reward.

## What is confirmed

- RSLOP total supply: 1,000,000,000 RSLOP.
- Contributor airdrop allocation: 30% of total supply (300,000,000 RSLOP).
- Liquidity pool allocation: 70% of total supply.
- Developer pre-allocation: 0%.
- Rewards are for contributors who submit PRs. There is no allocation
  for the owner or for developers.

## What will be published when confirmed

When the criteria are confirmed, this document will be updated to state:

- the evaluation criteria (technical value, originality, reproducible
  evidence, verification level — not PR count),
- the payment procedure,
- the selection results.

Until then, no criteria, procedure, or selection result is published,
and none should be assumed.

## What is not rewarded

- Duplicate PRs.
- Auto-generated low-quality PRs.
- Unverified work presented as a finished feature.

## Records

Contributor PRs and verification results are recorded in
`contributors.json` at the repository root, updated by the maintainer
when a PR is merged.
